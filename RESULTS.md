# Results

## Final finding adjudication

| finding_id | status | finding | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | SUPPORTED | Sustained congestion and abrupt breakdown are empirically different traffic phenomena on this corridor. | The sustained-state rule produced 1457 events, the LA abrupt-collapse rule 10, and the Caltrans spatial rule 7, with extremely low pairwise onset overlap. |
| F2 | SUPPORTED | Corridor-level abrupt-breakdown episode construction is robust to the temporal clustering rule. | 15-, 30-, and 45-minute clustering windows all produced 6 corridor episodes. |
| F3 | SUPPORTED | Midday sustained-congestion episodes last substantially longer than AM or PM episodes. | Median durations were 50 minutes AM, 170 midday, and 45 PM. The midday advantage remained positive after every leave-one-station and leave-one-date exclusion. |
| F4 | SUGGESTIVE | Sustained congestion is often persistent and has a long duration tail. | Median duration was 60 minutes and the 90th percentile was 355 minutes. |
| F5 | SUPPORTED_WITH_CAVEAT | Most corridor propagation classifications are stable, but spatial extent is not completely insensitive to the allowed lag window. | 4/6 episode classifications were unchanged across 30-, 60-, and 120-minute propagation windows. |
| F6 | SUPPORTED_METHOD | Using 100% observed coverage instead of 80% costs no selected-corridor observations. | 80% and 100% thresholds retained 164657 rows and 2540.1 simultaneous corridor hours. |
| F7 | SUGGESTIVE | Five minutes before abrupt collapse, matched-control differences are modest rather than a simple dramatic precursor signature. | Event/control medians were 58.10/61.95 mph, 419.0/443.5 veh/5 min, and 0.1087/0.1150 occupancy. |
| F8 | SUPPORTED_WITH_CAVEAT | Recovery does not follow one universal station-order direction across the observed corridor episodes. | Episode-level travel-order recovery correlations include both positive and negative values. |
| F9 | UNSUPPORTED_FOR_SUBSTANTIVE_CLAIM | The current product-limit probability curve should not be used as a substantive portfolio finding. | The maximum estimated probability is only 0.0188% and 4 of the 10 pre-breakdown flows are below 600 veh/h/lane. |

## Key numbers

- 1,457 sustained-congestion events.
- 10 abrupt LA-rule onsets.
- 6 corridor episodes.
- Median duration 60 min; p90 355 min.
- AM / Midday / PM medians 50 / 170 / 45 min.
- Propagation classification stability: 4/6 episodes.
