# Limitations

| limitation_id | category | limitation | effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | scope | The primary analysis covers one five-station I-10 East corridor, so findings should not be generalized to all Los Angeles freeways. | limits external validity |
| L2 | time | The study period is February through May 2026 rather than a full annual cycle. | seasonal conditions may be underrepresented |
| L3 | sample size | Only 10 LA-rule abrupt onsets and 6 corridor episodes were identified. | limits precision of abrupt-onset and propagation inference |
| L4 | event concentration | The most concentrated abrupt-onset date accounts for 40.0% of all LA-rule onsets. | precursor results are vulnerable to date-specific conditions |
| L5 | measurement | The analysis relies on loop-detector speed, flow, occupancy, and observed-percent fields rather than direct vehicle trajectories. | limits interpretation of microscopic traffic behavior |
| L6 | operational definitions | Sustained congestion and abrupt breakdown are defined with published operational rules, but no single universal breakdown definition exists. | results depend on the phenomenon being measured |
| L7 | causality | The study is observational and does not identify causal drivers such as incidents, demand shocks, weather, or signal operations. | findings describe dynamics rather than causes |
| L8 | probability modeling | The product-limit probability analysis has only ten uncensored abrupt-breakdown observations and includes implausibly low pre-breakdown flows for a capacity interpretation. | PLM result retained as audit evidence, not a headline finding |
